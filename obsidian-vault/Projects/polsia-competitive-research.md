# Polsia — competitive research note

Full source: `docs/COMPETITOR-POLSIA-DEEP-RESEARCH.md` in the repo; structured
in-app registry: `app/src/competitive-intel.js` (`window.PFV1_INTEL`).

## Why it matters
Polsia is the reference product, not the limit. The research says the real
opportunity is agents that are demonstrably reliable, accountable, economical
and recoverable — not simply more agents. That matches our existing free,
local-first, human-gated design.

## Key attributed figures
- Vendor charges reported: 3% payment fee, 20% ad fee, 31-day settlement,
  credits on $0/$20/$50/$100–200 plans. We deliberately have none of these.
- Founder-reported (Mixergy, 27 May 2026, unaudited): highest observed customer
  business $3k–$4k, ~50% first-month churn, no independently verified
  millionaires. No audited maximum was established.
- Trustpilot snapshot 3.5/5 over 482 self-selected reviews; deduplicated
  problem categories listed in the source doc.
- Contract-intelligence vertical: ~842 paying customers at £99/month for £1m
  ARR; ~4,210 for £1m operating profit at 20% margin. Unvalidated inputs.

## Adopted as reference
Five deployment modes, 100-area checklist, 12 benchmark targets, 10 audit
classifications, granular autonomy levels, hard limits on AI roles, self-healing
rules, and the adjacent-platform landscape (Paperclip, LangGraph, Temporal,
MCP/A2A, local model runtimes, decentralised infra). All labelled REFERENCE
unless independently verified locally.

## Not adopted
Paid gates, credits, revenue share, guaranteed returns, invented metrics,
blockchain as a requirement, copied proprietary code/assets/prompts.

## Open from this research
- No benchmark measurement harness for the 12 targets.
- No requirement-ID'd 100-area evidence table.
- No interchangeable agent-harness adapter runtime.
- Decentralised/self-hosted/centralised modes not provisioned.
- Contract-intelligence vertical not built; three-paid-pilot gate not met.
