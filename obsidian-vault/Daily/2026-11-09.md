## Per-conversation prompt caching (sacred)
Mutating past context / swapping toolsets mid-conversation breaks cached prefix (multiplies cost). Slash commands that change system-prompt state defer to next session unless `--now`. Our delegation (message_agent/subagent) avoids this via fresh child sessions. Compression (`/compress`) is the only OK.
